About Us

A considered approach to photography, from first conversation to final image.

MCM Photography is a Cape Town studio serving individuals, families, schools and businesses. We bring the same thoughtful preparation, technical care and attention to detail to every assignment, whether it is a portrait, a celebration or visual content for a brand.

We take time to understand the purpose of each shoot, guide clients through the process and create images that feel authentic to the people and occasion. From planning and photography to careful editing and delivery, our focus is on making the experience clear, welcoming and professional.
